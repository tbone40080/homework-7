About Me and the Game

When talking about my favorite game I like to point out the fact that I don't just stick to one specific role on Overwatch that's what most people call a flex queue meaning I'll play any role like Tank, DPS (Damage), or Support. I got into the game because it gave me a break from stress at home and from schoolwork like I am doing right now, and for a while it was and still is the one thing that reliably made me happy during the day. These days I keep playing to stay connected with friends and laugh through the chaos we get into together.

My Favorite Heroes

Zenyatta

Zenyatta lets me play Support without playing it safe. It's like playing DPS but as a Support because Zenyatta has two orbs one called the Harmony orb and another called a Discord orb. So that gives me the ability to both heal my team and play aggressively against enemies.

Reinhardt

Reinhardt is who I pick when I want to be the one making space for my team. I could push into the backlines of the enemies and distract them while my team gets to whichever objective there is. I also like to play Reinhardt because of his playstyle since I like being up close and personal and I can do that with the way he charges in and swings his big hammer around.
